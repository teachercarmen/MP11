Setup — before students arrive

1. Open this teacher dashboard on your computer (connected to the projector). Keep it on the Student cards tab so cards appear live as students submit.

2. Share globalSpark_student.html with students — via QR code, Google Classroom, Teams, or the class WhatsApp group. Students open it on their phones.

3. Both files use the key globalspark2526 to sync. Cards submitted by students in other browser tabs on the same computer appear here automatically. For cross-device sharing between student phones and your computer, host the files on a local network (see note below).

Cross-device tip: run python -m http.server 8000 in the folder with both files, then share your IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.x:8000). All devices on the school WiFi can then access both files and cards sync via the shared localStorage key.

Teacher notes

Setup: Project this on screen. Students open the link on their phones or use classroom computers. Each student fills in their own Talent Card (~8 min). Cards appear in the live gallery as they are submitted.

Team finding: Students browse the gallery, identify 2 colleagues with complementary skills, and introduce themselves in English using the conversation prompt on their card (~7 min).

Agency missions: Teams of 3 complete one of the three mini-briefs on Screen 4 (~10 min). Each team pitches their idea in 60 seconds to the class. Teacher selects the best pitch.

Total time: ~30–35 minutes. No accounts needed. Cards reset when the page is refreshed.

1
0–2 min
Set the scene

Project this dashboard. Tell students: "You are now employees of GlobalSpark — a Madrid-based international marketing agency. Before you start work, the HR team needs your profile. Open the link I'm sharing and fill in your Talent Card. In English."

2
2–10 min · Individual
Students fill in their Talent Card

Watch cards appear on your screen live. Encourage students to open the Vocabulary panel if stuck. Circulate and prompt: "What role fits you best?" "What's something you can do that most people can't?" Remind them: short and honest beats long and invented.

3
10–17 min · Pair/group
Browse the gallery — find your team

Switch your projector to the Student cards tab. Students browse the gallery on their phones to find 2 colleagues with complementary skills. They must introduce themselves in English using the guided steps in the app. Circulate: "What's their role?" "Why did you choose them?"

4
17–27 min · Teams of 3
Agency Mission — prepare and pitch

Assign each team a brief (A, B or C — visible in their app). Go to the Form groups tab and project the teams. Teams use the Pitch Builder to prepare. Tell them: "You have 5 minutes. Then 60 seconds to pitch. Use the structure in your app. Go."

5
27–35 min · Class
Pitches + debrief

Each team pitches in 60 seconds. Class votes: most creative / most realistic / best tagline. Introduce key vocabulary: target audience, brand voice, USP, call to action, engagement. Close: "These clients — GreenLife Organics, La Tienda del Campo — you'll see them again all year. You've just started building their campaigns."

Conversation task

Find someone with a skill you don't have. Introduce yourself in English using this structure:

"Hi, I'm [name]. I'm the [role] at GlobalSpark. My superpower is [superpower]. I noticed your skill in [their skill] — I think we could work well together because [reason]. What's your ambition here?"

Debrief notes

After the pitches: Ask the class — which pitch was most creative? Which was most realistic? Which had the best tagline? Use real marketing vocabulary: target audience, brand voice, USP, call to action.

The names on these briefs (GreenLife Organics, La Tienda del Campo) will appear again throughout this course — they are GlobalSpark's permanent clients. Students are already building their professional context for the year.
