💬 How to introduce yourself — step by step guide

Tap to open ▼

Find a colleague with a skill you don't have. Use these steps. You don't need to memorise them — just follow them in order.

1
Greet and introduce yourself
"Hi! I'm your name. I'm the your role here at GlobalSpark."
Also useful: "Nice to meet you." · "Good morning!" · "Hey, I don't think we've met yet."
2
Say where you're from
"I'm from your city. What about you?"
Also useful: "I grew up in..." · "I'm originally from... but I live in..."
3
Share your superpower
"My superpower is your superpower. That's why I joined as a your role."
Also useful: "I'm especially good at..." · "People always tell me I'm good at..." · "I can..."
4
Show interest in their skills
"I saw your card — your their skill skills are really strong. I think we could work well together because give a reason."
Also useful: "That's impressive!" · "I'd love to learn more about that." · "I don't have that skill — I need someone like you!"
5
Ask about their ambition
"What's your ambition at GlobalSpark this year?"
Also useful: "What do you want to get out of this course?" · "What kind of campaigns do you want to work on?" · "What are you most excited about?"
6
Close the conversation
"Great to meet you, their name. Let's work together on the mission."
Also useful: "See you around!" · "I'll find you when we form teams." · "I think we'd make a good team."
Remember: Don't worry about perfect grammar. Use the phrases above and focus on communicating. If you don't know a word, describe it in English — that's what professional communicators do.

Agency Missions

Your first client briefs. Work with your team. You have 5 minutes. Then pitch in 60 seconds.

🎤 How to build your 60-second pitch — guided structure

Tap to open ▼

Your team has 5 minutes to prepare. Use this structure. Each person can take one or two steps. You don't need slides — just speak clearly.

1
⏱ 10 seconds
Hook — grab attention immediately
"Every day, [surprising fact or number]. That's the world our client is entering."
Other options: Start with a question — "How many of you bought something because of an Instagram post?" · Start with a problem — "The problem is nobody knows this brand exists. Yet."
2
⏱ 10 seconds
Client & challenge
"Our client is [client name]. They [one sentence about what they do]. The challenge: [the problem to solve]."
Useful words: target audience · brand awareness · online presence · market · competitors
3
⏱ 20 seconds
Our idea — the campaign concept
"Our campaign is called [campaign name]. The tagline is: [tagline — 6 words or fewer]. The idea is [explain in 2 sentences]."
Useful words: campaign · concept · visual · message · content · launch · platform · hashtag · viral · engagement
4
⏱ 10 seconds
Why it will work
"This works because [reason — connect to the target audience]. Our target audience is [who] and they [what they care about]."
Useful words: resonate with · connect with · appeal to · relevant to · authentic · trust · values · lifestyle
5
⏱ 10 seconds
Call to action — close strong
"We are GlobalSpark. We make brands [one powerful word]. Give us this brief — we'll make it happen."
Other closings: "Thank you — any questions?" · "We're ready to start tomorrow." · "This campaign will put [client] on the map."
🗣 Delivery tips

Speak slowly — especially in English. Look at your audience, not at your notes. One person speaks at a time. Practice once before you pitch.

⚠️ Common mistakes

Don't read from your phone. Don't say "ummm" — pause instead. Don't apologise for your English. Don't go over 60 seconds.

📝 Prepare in 5 minutes

1. Read the brief together (1 min). 2. Agree on the idea (2 min). 3. Assign each person a step from the structure above (1 min). 4. Quick run-through (1 min). Done.

✅ What makes a great pitch

A clear idea · a memorable tagline · a specific target audience · at least one concrete detail (a number, a platform, an image). Confidence beats perfection.

The GreenLife Brief

GreenLife Organics makes sustainable plant-based snacks. They want to launch on Instagram and TikTok for the first time. They have a budget of €5,000. Their target audience is 18–30 year-olds in Spanish cities who care about the environment but also want food that tastes good.

Your task: Come up with a campaign name, a tagline (under 6 words), and ONE specific idea for their first piece of content. What would you post? What would it look like?

The Crisis Brief

Your task: What do you do in the next 60 minutes? Give three specific actions in order of priority. Decide who does what. Draft the first sentence of the public apology post in English.

The New Client Brief

A traditional Spanish olive oil producer from Jaén — La Tienda del Campo — wants to sell to UK supermarkets for the first time. They have never done marketing in English. They don't know what makes them different from any other olive oil.

Your task: Find ONE thing that makes them special (you can invent it). Create a name for their UK product line, a tagline in English, and describe the image for their packaging. What would you put on the label?
