The story of the year

You are not studying English. You are working in English — as part of a real international marketing agency. GlobalSpark is headquartered in Madrid and works with international clients. Every block of this course is a professional assignment for the agency.

You will write cover letters, pitch campaigns, manage client crises, go global, and deal with a very unhappy customer. By the end of the year, you will have produced real professional documents — in English — for real professional situations. The grammar and vocabulary come from the book. The context comes from the agency.

Your permanent clients this year:

🌱 GreenLife Organics — sustainable food brand 🫒 La Tienda del Campo — traditional Spanish food producer 🛒 ShopNow.es — e-commerce client

How the blocks connect
The blocks are not independent. They follow a single agency story:

The trend you defend in B2 becomes the strategic insight for the GreenLife brief in B1. The bank meeting minutes from B4 appear in the Term 1 radio programme. The campaign you analyse in B5 informs the cultural adaptation you do in B7. The client you rescue in B8 is the same agency whose brochure you wrote in B7. And the Big Pitch in Term 2 is GlobalSpark proving — after a full year — that it can deliver a complete, professional campaign from brief to broadcast.
